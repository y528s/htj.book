After a frustrating day of travel delays, rerouting, and lost bags, I have touched down in the UK. Not in itself worthy of a post, but this trip is the start of something new.

For a few years now I have watched BNJC revitalise Jewish life in Brighton and Hove. This Jewish community centre stands on the site of my old school, the place that gave me the foundation to become the Jew I am today. It is a pleasure to join the team as Head of Marketing, Brand, Communications and Performance. In this role I will be combining my professional skills with my community development and volunteerism.

And to my friends who are Jewish community professionals in the UK and around the world, if you think there is something we could do together, get in touch. Programming or partnership, Shabbatons or seaside. My inbox is open.

Wonderful things to follow. Watch this space.
